\section{Method}
Consider the objective
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}(\theta) = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}
  \ell\!\left(f_\theta(x_i), y_i\right) + \lambda \|\theta\|_2^2,
\end{equation}
where $f_\theta$ is the model and $\ell$ the loss function.
